How to Play

1. Start a new game. Name your airline, optionally pick a logo, then buy your first airport — that's your home base.

2. Buy aircraft. Open your home airport's Fleet Shop to add planes suited to the routes you want to fly.

3. Create routes. From an aircraft in storage, pick a destination airport, shape the flight path, then set fares and a daily departure schedule.

4. Watch it run. Flights operate in real time, even while you're away — progress picks up right where it left off when you return.

5. Grow your airline. Reinvest profits into more aircraft, new airports, and airport upgrades. Keep an eye on reputation — fair pricing keeps passengers happy.
